FH1-nx installer

Official sources only: this page and github.com/KazekiKitten/fh1-nx/releases. FH1-nx is free and never comes with the game's files. If you paid for it, or got it as an NSP with the game inside, it's a pirated repack: not official, possibly modified, and not supported.

Makes the FH1-nx package from your own copy of Forza Horizon. Everything runs in this browser: your game files never get uploaded anywhere.

Your game

You need the USA retail disc (MS-2505), as an ISO or as the extracted folder with default.xex and media.

Then

Extract the zip into sdmc:/switch/. You should end up with:

/switch/fh1-nx/
  fh1nx.nro
  deko3d_glsl.bin       (shaders)
  fh1_shaders.nfsspv    (shaders)
  shadercache_dksh.bin  (the shaders, compiled for the Switch)
  game_root/            (your game files)

Start it with a title override (hold R while launching a game) or a forwarder set to a 39-bit address space. Album/applet mode doesn't have enough memory. fh1nx.toml (settings) gets written on the first start.

The game files are copied into the zip (about 7 GB) as it downloads. Keep this tab open until the download finishes. Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

FH1-nx is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Microsoft, Playground Games or Turn 10. The game files come from your own copy. Source: github.com/KazekiKitten/fh1-nx (GPL-3.0).
